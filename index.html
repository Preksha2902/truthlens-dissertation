About TruthLens

TruthLens is a comparative multimodal framework for detecting AI-generated content across text, image, and audio. Each modality uses independently trained deep learning models evaluated on publicly available benchmark datasets.

The framework addresses a critical gap in existing research - most detection tools handle only one content type. TruthLens evaluates all three modalities under a consistent framework, enabling direct cross-modal performance comparison, and reports honestly where a modality's real-world generalisation is still limited.

📝 Text

A single RoBERTa-base classifier fine-tuned on HC3, cross-evaluated on RAID for out-of-distribution generalisation. Detects low perplexity, uniform sentence structure, and hedging language patterns.

RoBERTa-baseHC3RAID
🖼 Image

Two ResNet-50 models: one for portrait/face photos, one for general images, plus automatic face-detection routing between them. Both were originally trained on CIFAKE and then fine-tuned - the general model on OpenFake - after CIFAKE alone proved to be a dataset-artifact shortcut rather than a genuine real-vs-AI signal on real-world photos. Two ViT-B/16 variants exist as an experimental comparison.

ResNet-50ViT-B/16CIFAKEOpenFake
🎵 Audio

Wav2Vec2 and MFCC+CNN fine-tuned on ASVspoof 2019 with genuine codec-compression augmentation (v3). Four successive training attempts improved benchmark scores, but real-world generalisation to unseen recording conditions remains an open problem, honestly reported rather than hidden.

Benchmark and real-world results diverge in both directions here, not just one. Audio's final models (v3) score 96-99% on held-out data yet still misclassify a real voice recording. The general-image fine-tune shows the opposite pattern: only 70.00% on its own OpenFake validation split, yet 4/4 correct on independently-sourced real-world photos. Both are reported as measured, not smoothed into a single "it works" number. See "About TruthLens" for details.
